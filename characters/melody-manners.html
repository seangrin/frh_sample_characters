Melody Manners

Beautiful, socially fluid, and faintly unreal even before anyone guesses how much faerie blood is moving under the surface - a liminal operator who turns glamour into access and access into leverage, with a 469-point faerie guardian lion that follows her everywhere she goes.

Points: 3741/2 Age: 28 Height/Weight: 5'2", 115 lbs Public Read: Performer and socialite - Savoir-Faire (Show Business)-20, Bard-19, Beautiful. The public version presents as charming, fate-believing, and socially effortless. The faerie blood, the guardian, and the full skill depth are all concealed behind that surface. Core Role: Social infiltrator, performer, faerie-touched operative; Chuff's anchor; Weirdness Magnet with teeth

Powers

  • Ally (Faerie Lion - Chuff) (451-500 points, 15 or less) [135]: the dominant mechanical fact of her sheet. Chuff is 469 points of faerie guardian - DR 14, Invisibility, Insubstantiality, Super Running 31.625 mph, Sonic Blast (sonic 3d, Area Effect, Range 30), Claws (imp 4d-1/cut 6d+1), Monstrous. He appears on rolls of 15 or less. This Ally advantage is the architectural center of her build: 135 points is a substantial fraction of her total spend, and it buys the closest thing FRH has to an invisible supernatural bodyguard.
  • Special Rapport [10]: the mechanical link between Melody and Chuff. The rapport is mutual - it explains why Chuff's loyalty is directional and total rather than generic protection-beast behavior.
  • Bard-19* [4]: modified by Charisma and Animal Empathy. Her performance capability reaches IQ+3 with cost modifiers. In show business, social, and magical contexts, Bard functions as a general-purpose charisma multiplier.
  • Fortune Telling-18* [4]: a faerie-blood gift expressed as skill - reading fate, trend, and near-future probability through the cultural form of performance. At IQ+2 with cost modifiers, this is not a casual parlor act.
  • Danger Sense [15]: threat awareness before visible indicators. Backed by Chuff's presence, this means she rarely enters a dangerous situation without at least one layer of supernatural early warning.
  • Staurophobia (Mild) [-10] and Vulnerability 1 (Pure Iron) (Occasional) [-10]: intrinsic consequences of the faerie blood. Crosses cause mild fear. Pure iron causes vulnerability. These are not accidents - they are the signature of what she is.

Required Secret Power

Two layered identity secrets (grouped):

  • Secret (a real Faerie) [-20] - Chuff himself is the visible proof. His presence near Melody is the undeniable sign that something genuinely faerie is attached to her life. Whether this secret refers to Chuff's nature or to something deeper about Melody's own identity is a campaign-level question the sheet leaves open.
  • Secret (One-quarter Faerie by Blood) [-30] - the heritage secret. Grandmother was a Half-Faerie Witch; Melody is the quarter-faerie granddaughter. The Staurophobia, Vulnerability (Pure Iron), Unusual Background, Weirdness Magnet, and Special Rapport with Chuff all flow from this origin.

The two secrets are distinct in severity: -30 is the bloodline secret; -20 is the real-faerie secret. Together they define her as something other than human even before anyone has met Chuff.

Required Secret Society

The Faerie.

Secret (a real Faerie) [-20] is the secret-society entry. The Faerie are a real society in the FRH sense - an organized, non-human order with its own agenda, loyalties, and demands, accessed through blood and bond rather than initiation. Melody's quarter-faerie heritage and her bond with Chuff constitute membership in that order whether she sought it or not.

This is distinct from a fraternal or human secret society. The concealment obligation is the same - exposure ends her ability to operate in the ordinary world - but the organization she belongs to predates and supersedes human institutional structures. FRH does not satisfy this requirement.

How Melody Operates

  • Savoir-Faire (Show Business)-20 is the highest skill on her sheet and the foundation of her social access. In entertainment, performance, and high-society contexts, she operates at IQ+4 - a skill level that puts her beyond most human practitioners. She is genuinely exceptional at the surface she presents.
  • Bard-19 and Fortune Telling-18 give her two performance-grade social capabilities with genuine predictive and persuasive depth. Fortune Telling in a 1923 investigative context is not decoration - it is a real-world intelligence gathering tool that doubles as cover for whatever the faerie gifts are actually doing.
  • Guns/TL6 (Pistol)-18 is the second-highest explicitly offensive skill on the sheet after Bard-19. The Remington Double-Derringer and Smith and Wesson .38 Special are both in her equipment. She can shoot, and she can shoot well.
  • Chuff's availability on a 15-or-less roll makes him present in almost every scene. He can use Invisibility (carry Light Encumbrance) and Insubstantiality (same) - meaning he can shadow Melody invisibly, pass through walls, and emerge in a crisis without warning. For enemies who don't know Chuff exists, this is operationally decisive.
  • Weirdness Magnet [-15] is both a liability and a narrative engine. Strange events cluster around Melody. In an FRH investigation context, this means she does not have to look for the supernatural - it finds her. The cost is that it also finds everyone near her.
  • The Staurophobia and Vulnerability (Pure Iron) are management obligations. Crossed environments (churches, missions, roadside shrines) cause a Mild fear reaction. Contact with pure iron is an active mechanical vulnerability, not merely thematic. Both share with Chuff, who has the same disadvantages.

Why She Works

Melody works in FRH because glamour and teeth are the same thing in her sheet. The social skills that make her charming are backed by a 469-point supernatural guardian and two identity secrets that would end her public life if exposed. She is not only a face character - she is a face character with a faerie on call, a vulnerability to iron, and a compulsion to attract the inexplicable. The combination makes every social scene she enters carry genuine stakes on both sides of the conversation.

Character Overview

Melody Manners survives by being exactly the sort of person human beings underestimate until it is too late. She is beautiful, socially fluid, fate-leaning, and faintly unreal even before anyone guesses how much faerie blood is actually moving under the surface. Melody knows how to charm a room, redirect a conversation, make a lie feel more comfortable than the truth, and move through the kinds of spaces where performance, seduction, danger, and opportunity all share a wall. She is not only decorative and not only a con artist. She is a liminal operator, someone built to turn glamour into access and access into leverage.

That would already be enough to make her risky. Chuff is what makes her impossible to mistake for anything ordinary. He is not a separate free-floating companion so much as the inhuman truth that lives too close to her life to ever be excluded from it. He only works because he can remain near her, hide when necessary, survive what should not be survivable, and protect both Melody and the secrets around her. Together they create a pressure field rather than merely a duo: the socially legible fae-touched woman and the creature who proves the strangeness is not metaphorical.

For a new player, Melody should feel like someone who can talk her way into delicate spaces and survive the consequences only because she was never entirely human to begin with. Chuff should feel less like a pet or mascot and more like the protective, half-hidden shadow of that same truth. They belong together because the story they tell is one story: glamour with teeth, secrecy with loyalty, and the unsettling knowledge that the thing keeping her alive is also one of the clearest signs that she does not belong fully to the ordinary world anymore.
