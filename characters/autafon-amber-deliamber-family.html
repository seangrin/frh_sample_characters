Autafon "Amber" Deliamber

Disciplined occult helper, healer, sensor, and moral center whose hidden training makes her one of the party's most precise forms of supernatural support.

Powers

  • Psionic sensitivity: Amber can sense, diagnose, and quietly influence hidden conditions other people cannot even perceive.
  • Psionic healing: she can stabilize, restore, and keep people functioning when the group would otherwise start breaking down.
  • Practical occult magic: her spell list emphasizes visibility control, perception, support, and healing rather than flashy destruction.
  • Martial restraint: even without an overt cinematic martial-arts secret package, she is physically disciplined enough to protect herself when pressure becomes personal.

Enemy (Hunter of Lotus Bells) [-10]: the secret society is not merely flavor. It has social and predatory consequences in the world.

How Amber Uses Her Powers

  • She senses fear, lies, sickness, spiritual wrongness, hidden states, and metaphysical pressure before other characters have enough information to react correctly.
  • She keeps the party alive with healing, triage, and physiological control rather than by overwhelming scenes with spectacle.
  • She uses utility and perception magic to clarify danger, move quietly through the strange, and strengthen the group's control over uncertain situations.
  • She combines law, accounting, medicine, street sense, and occult understanding so her supernatural gifts stay grounded in practical investigation.
  • She works best when the problem is subtle, dangerous, emotionally charged, spiritually contaminated, or likely to injure people faster than they understand what is happening.

Character Overview

Autafon "Amber" Deliamber learned early that being visibly decent does not make the world gentle. As a half-Chinese woman moving through the hard edges of respectability, bureaucracy, and ordinary prejudice, she grew into the habit of watchfulness long before she understood that some of what she noticed was not ordinary at all. People in pain drew her eye. Fear arrived in rooms before anyone spoke it aloud. Sickness, lies, spiritual wrongness, and the small disturbances that others ignored seemed to announce themselves to her whether she wanted them to or not. By the time she came under the influence of the Lotus Bell, she was already the kind of person who could not help noticing hurt, danger, and hidden disorder.

The Lotus Bell did not make Amber into something she had never been. It gave discipline, language, and obligation to gifts that were already pushing at the edges of her life. Under their training she learned medicine, herbs, quiet spiritual practice, self-command, and the careful use of certain powers and special abilities that were never meant to be displayed carelessly before the world. Some of those talents seemed almost miraculous at a sickbed. Some let her sense what others missed. Some made her unnervingly hard to surprise or deceive. None of them came free. They tied her to a hidden order, made secrecy a permanent condition of her life, and put enemies on her trail.

What makes Amber valuable in play is that she is not built like a swaggering wonder-worker or a casual fighter. She is a conscientious occult helper: a healer, sensor, and moral center who can patch wounds, read danger, notice what has gone wrong, and keep moving when other people start to panic. She knows enough law, accounting, medicine, street sense, and occult craft to function in the ordinary world while never really belonging to it. She dresses conservatively, keeps her habits disciplined, and carries herself like someone who expects the day to ask more of her than it will of other people. Under that reserve is a woman with a readable conscience, a practiced calm, and just enough strange ability to make everyone around her safer, or far more uneasy, depending on what exactly she has noticed.
