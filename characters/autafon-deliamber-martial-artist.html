Autafon "Amber" Deliamber

Martial Artist Build

The build where the Lotus Bell's Judo training runs deep - Judo-18, Power Blow-15, Pressure Points-17, Pressure Secrets-15, Blinding Touch, and Hit Location targeting combined with the full healing and sensing psi suite.

Powers - Martial Artist Build

  • ESP 5 [15] and Telepathy 5 [10]: the full sensing suite - Clairaudience-16, Clairvoyance-16, Precognition-16, Emotion Sense-15, Sense Aura-16, Detect Lies-18. Identical to the Dragon Breath build at this power level.
  • Psionic Healing 15 [45]: Psionic Healing-18. The healing core is constant across all three builds.
  • Judo-18 [8] - Parry 13: the combat distinction of this build. Higher by 1 point than the Dragon Breath build's Judo-17, and producing a Parry of 13 - the highest of the three configurations. At DX+1, this is trained Lotus Bell close-combat discipline at its deepest.
  • Power Blow-15, Pressure Points-17, Pressure Secrets-15: cinematic martial arts capabilities. Power Blow multiplies strike damage. Pressure Points targets specific anatomical vulnerabilities. Pressure Secrets unlocks the most dangerous striking knowledge in the close-combat tradition.
  • Blinding Touch-14 with Hit Location (Eye) targeting: the capacity to permanently or temporarily disable a target's vision through precise striking. At Hit Location (Forehead) for Karate with Blinding Touch, the targeting architecture is deliberate and trained rather than improvised.

Required Secret Power

Psionic gifts and martial arts depth (grouped):

  • ESP 5 [15] - clairvoyance, precognition, aura sensing
  • Telepathy 5 [10] - emotion sense, detect lies at psionic depth
  • Psionic Healing 15 [45] - the healing capacity

The martial arts cinematic skills (Power Blow, Pressure Points/Secrets) likely fall under the Lotus Bell secrecy obligation alongside the psionic gifts - they are trained disciplines that should not be publicly demonstrated.

Required Secret Society

The Lotus Bell.

Secret (Member of The Lotus Bell) [-20] is the society entry. The Lotus Bell is the non-FRH hidden order. The affiliation is expressed through the Secret disadvantage; a formal Patron advantage is not required. FRH does not count toward this requirement.

How This Build's Powers Are Used

  • Judo-18 and Parry 13 make this build the most capable in close-grappling scenarios. In a 1923 context where violence often happens in small rooms, doorways, and tight social spaces, a Parry of 13 backed by Lotus Bell discipline is operationally significant.
  • Power Blow at skill 15 can multiply unarmed strike damage. Combined with Karate-16 and the anatomical targeting suite, Amber can deliver force well above what her ST 9 would normally suggest.
  • Pressure Points-17 and Pressure Secrets-15 are the most dangerous tools in the close-combat kit. They require contact-range positioning but bypass armor and produce results (pain, paralysis, damage) through knowledge rather than strength.
  • Blinding Touch with Hit Location (Eye) targeting is available as a disabling option - reserved for situations where disabling a threat without lethal force is needed but ordinary grappling is insufficient.
  • The full psionic suite operates identically to the Dragon Breath build: Detect Lies-18, Emotion Sense-15, Clairvoyance-16, Precognition-16, Sense Aura-16, Psionic Healing-18. The build choice does not change her sensing and healing capability.
  • Pacifism (Cannot Harm Innocents) constrains all combat options including the martial arts suite. The targeting knowledge is not license - she will not use it against noncombatants.

Character Overview

Autafon "Amber" Deliamber learned early that being visibly decent does not make the world gentle. The Lotus Bell did not make her into something she had never been - it gave discipline, language, and obligation to gifts that were already pushing at the edges of her life. In the Martial Artist build, that discipline runs deep into the body: the Lotus Bell training produced not only a healer and sensor but someone who can enter close contact with a threat, read its intent through Emotion Sense-15 and Detect Lies-18 while it is happening, and respond through Judo-18 with a Parry of 13 before the threat finishes forming.

What makes Amber valuable in play is that she is not built like a swaggering wonder-worker. She is a conscientious occult helper - healer, sensor, moral center - and in this configuration she is also the last line of protection if everything goes wrong in a doorway, a corridor, or the wrong kind of room. She dresses conservatively and carries herself like someone who expects the day to ask more of her than it will of other people. The Pressure Secrets training is not visible from the outside until the moment it becomes necessary.
