Lucien Wei Mercier

Cosmopolitan chi-master and disciplined martialist who is most frightening when he has had one breath to settle into control - elegance backed by exactness.

Powers

  • DX 19 [150]: the highest Dexterity in the character library at this point level. This is not merely excellent - it is definitively supernormal. Everything Lucien does with his body is powered by a physical platform that no training alone can explain.
  • Trained By A Master [40] with cinematic Jujutsu: Power Blow-15, Pressure Points-16, Pressure Secrets-13, Flying Leap-16, and extensive maneuver library. He can stop threats, disarm them, or neutralize them through precision technique rather than force.
  • Enhanced Dodge [15] and Perfect Balance [15]: he is nearly impossible to hit and does not fall down. Combined with DX 19, his defense is extraordinary under any circumstance.
  • Weirdness Magnet [-15]: the uncanny arrives dressed well around Lucien. He does not cause the strange - he attracts it, which makes him an early-warning system in metaphysically active cases.

  • Enemy (Unknown Enemies of FRH) [-60]: Unknown: -5; Subtraction (Access to Magic in Non-magical World): -15.

Required Secret Power

Chi-supernormal (grouped):

  • DX 19 [150] - the physical anchor of the hidden lane; no natural human being reaches this level
  • Trained By A Master [40] - the martial framework that channels the chi into operational technique
  • Perfect Balance [15] - body mastery at a level that implies more than practice
  • Enhanced Dodge [15] and Enhanced Parry (Bare Hands) [6] - reflexive defense beyond the human range
  • Power Blow-15, Pressure Points-16, Pressure Secrets-13, Flying Leap-16 - the cinematic skill expressions that make the chi operationally visible

Secret (Chi-supernormal in a non-magical world) [-20]: the real source of his abilities is hidden. He reads as extraordinarily skilled, not as what he actually is.

Required Secret Society

The Chi Masters. The hidden tradition that trained Lucien — a lineage of chi-supernormal martial practitioners who move through the underworld of three continents without advertising their existence.

Patron (Chi Masters) (6 or less) [5]: Secret: -5. Membership is not disclosed; in a world that does not acknowledge chi as real, the tradition protects itself through anonymity. FRH does not count toward this requirement.

How Lucien Uses His Powers

  • He is the party's combat ceiling - the person who can end a physical confrontation faster and more decisively than anyone else present, through technique rather than blunt force.
  • His social toolkit (four languages, multiple Savoir-Faire specialties, Reputation, Diplomacy) means he can operate in cosmopolitan upper-class, martial underworld, and Chinese community contexts that most FRH investigators cannot navigate.
  • His Weirdness Magnet status makes him metaphysically significant in ways he may not fully understand. Cases that brush the supernatural tend to orient toward him before they become visible to others.
  • He is most frightening in scenes where he has had one moment to settle. Overconfidence means he sometimes presses advantages too far - a genuine tactical liability that creates good dramatic risk.
  • Hypnotic Hands-14 and Hypnotism-13 give him non-violent dominance options that most physical specialists lack entirely.

Why He Works

Lucien proves that a chi-master reads best when he is focused rather than broad - frightening precisely because he is exact, not because he is everything. His elegance is not decoration. It is the shape danger has taken inside him after a lifetime of discipline.

Character Overview

Lucien Wei Mercier was built for discipline before he was built for wonder. He moves through the world with the polished inevitability of a man whose body, manners, and attention have all been trained to answer the same command at once. He is multilingual, impeccably balanced, weapon-fast, and socially exact - the sort of person who can enter a room already carrying enough refinement to make most men decide they have misjudged their own posture. That polish is part French, part Chinese, part cosmopolitan survival, and part the old martial truth that elegance can be more intimidating than brute force if it is backed by enough exactness.

The early temptation with Lucien was to make him too broad, too much of a four-color chi-master who could be everything at once. The better version of him is narrower and more dangerous. Lucien is terrifying because he is focused. He does not merely fight well; he becomes most frightening when he has had one breath, one beat, one moment to settle into control. His miracles are not casual. They are the flowering of preparation, composure, and impeccable timing. That keeps him human enough to remain interesting and strange enough to imply a much larger martial-metaphysical world standing just offstage.

That wider world matters because Lucien carries more than physical skill. He carries etiquette, codes, hidden lineages of discipline, and the social ability to disarm people before he ever has to disarm their weapons. His refinement is not the opposite of danger. It is the shape danger has taken inside him. Weirdness still follows him, which is part of why he belongs in FRH rather than in a cleaner pulp universe where excellence is reward enough. His gift does not free him from the uncanny; it makes the uncanny arrive dressed well.

For a new player, Lucien should feel almost superhuman without ever becoming merely a glamorous answer to every problem. He is a disciplined chi-master, frightening when calm, socially polished, and always one step away from implying a larger and stranger scale than the room may be ready to contain.
