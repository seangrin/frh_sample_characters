Julian Ashcroft

Powers

  • Psychometry 8 [8] - 10,648-year reach (8,725 BCE), 484" radius: Julian reads object history at a depth that makes him an unparalleled archive investigator. Handled objects within investigative relevance yield direct access to custody, use, and events. With extra effort (+4 power, -12 fatigue, -8 skill), reach extends to 17,576 years. In a world of forged documents, disputed collections, and objects whose custody tells the real story, this is operationally decisive.
  • Telekinesis 6 [24] - fine manipulation: the same quarter-coin-at-8-mph capability as the 1923 Tiberius, but Julian uses it with the precision of a man who understands locks, seals, and document weight. He does not lift tables. He moves the one precisely positioned object that collapses a system.
  • Psychometry-17 and Telekinesis-17 (from skills): both psionic skills are trained to high proficiency, not left at base power. Julian does not merely have these gifts - he has cultivated them as professional tools.
  • Acting-18, Forgery-17, Disguise-16, Fast-Talk-18, Stealth-16: the non-supernatural layer. His social infiltration and document-manipulation competence would make him exceptional even without the psionic backing. The two layers reinforce each other: the forgery tells him what the document should look like; the psychometry tells him what it actually carries.

Required Secret Society

No named non-FRH Patron or secret-society affiliation appears in the source sheet.

Julian's single disadvantage is FRH Duty. He carries no Secret naming a society, no second Patron, and no organizational affiliation beyond FRH and his private moral architecture. For a gentleman thief operating in 1870 elite and auction-circuit society, the absence of a named hidden order is notable. His network is relational - clubs, country houses, auction porters, railway clerks - rather than institutional.

This may be the correct design: Julian belongs to the world he robs, not to an order within it. Or it may be a 1870-party gap. The current page preserves the source as written.

FRH does not satisfy the secret-society requirement.

How Julian Uses His Powers

  • Psychometry-17 in practice means he can pick up a handled document, a cabinet key, or a collection piece and receive direct residue of who held it and when. In an investigation built around disputed ownership, stolen archives, forged provenance, or substituted artifacts, this makes the physical evidence itself a witness.
  • Telekinesis-17 at fine-manipulation precision means lock mechanisms, document flaps, seal wax, and cabinet catches are all within reach. He can interfere with physical systems from a few feet away without leaving prints, without making noise, and without being present in the obvious sense.
  • Acting-18 and Disguise-16 at these levels mean he is one of the most capable social impersonators in the historical party library - comparable to the 1923 Adrian Draik. He can build a cover identity, hold it under pressure, and exit it cleanly.
  • Forgery-17 with Calligraphy-15 and Pickpocket-14 means the document-and-access layer is not just conceptual - he can produce the forgeries, acquire the originals, and understand what makes a document legitimate from the inside.
  • Savoir-Faire (High Society)-19 backed by Status 2, Handsome, Voice, and Charisma +1 means he can go anywhere in 1870 elite society without friction. The social access is not a minor convenience - it is the operating environment he is designed for.

Why He Works

Julian proves that a psychic investigator in the 1870 context reads sharpest when the psionic lane is built around touch, residue, and the one precisely moved thing that collapses certainty. He does not announce power loudly. He only needs to know which document was substituted, which handled object carries the wrong story, and which quarter-inch shift will make a system betray itself at the exact wrong moment for its owner.

Character Overview

Julian Ashcroft became a criminal by studying the respectable version first. He learned early that ownership is often theater, that collections are arguments about power disguised as taste, and that one well-placed document can move a crate, a railcar, an inheritance, a museum cabinet, or a man's conscience more efficiently than open force ever could. So he cultivated grace, not because he was innocent, but because elegance opens doors. He cultivated patience because cabinets, seals, invitation lists, and porter chains all reward the person willing to wait one step longer than everyone else. By 1870 he has made himself into an artist of false legitimacy.

That is why his investigative profile reads so sharply. Julian is not merely a gentleman thief. He is a provenance predator and paper-haunting operator whose battlefield is access, altered authorship, cabinet logic, keys, seals, and social permission. Psychometry and light telekinesis suit him perfectly because his supernatural life is built around touch, residue, and the one precisely moved thing that collapses certainty.

Morally, Julian is most interesting when he is not clean. He believes, with some justice, that much respectable property is already theft with older seals and better furniture. That conviction gives him both method and excuse. He can charm because charm is useful, not because he trusts the social order he navigates. He can admire beauty and still rob the collector. He can move through elite rooms and remain fundamentally illegitimate inside them.

For a new companion, Julian should feel dry, observant, elegant, and faintly amused by the pomposity of people who think paperwork makes them righteous. He is not a swashbuckler in a better coat. He is a man who understands that history can be stolen one page at a time and has built a life around deciding who deserves to lose the page.
