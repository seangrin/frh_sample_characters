Siegfried Vane

Dragonborn sorcerer living under a glamour amulet - bronze-red scales beneath a human face, Magery 3 fire and illusion, and the self-command of a man who knows exactly how much force he keeps leashed.

Points: 375 Age: 36 Public Read: Controlled, multilingual gentleman with an almost reptilian stillness - remarkable when the glamour amulet holds, catastrophic when it does not Core Role: Fire and illusion magic specialist, investigation-layer sorcerer, concealed nonhuman anchor

Powers

  • Magery 3 [35]: a full-power magic capacity expressed primarily through fire and illusion/perception spells. His spell list reads as a natural extension of draconic inheritance - fire, light, concealment, and truth-seeing are all at skill 19.
  • Dragon Amulet (Illusion 18, Power 3) [29]: the magical device that sustains his human face. It is breakable (DR ≤ 15 / HP ≤ 75), can be hit (-6 penalty), and can be stolen by stealth or trickery. If it breaks, the glamour drops and the room sees what he actually is.
  • DR 8 (Everything) + DR 15 (Fire): exceptional physical resilience and near-immunity to fire damage reflect the draconic nature underneath the illusion.
  • Eidetic Memory 2 [60]: this is an extraordinary advantage at this cost - deep retention that compounds his investigative and arcane capabilities significantly.

  • Enemy (Unknown Enemies of FRH) [-60]: Unknown: -5; Subtraction (Access to Magic in Non-magical World): -15.

Required Secret Power

Dragonborn nature and magic (grouped):

  • Magery 3 [35] - the full magical capacity of draconic inheritance
  • Dragon Amulet (Illusion 18, Power 3) [29] - the device that sustains the human glamour; its vulnerability is a structural weakness in his cover
  • DR 8 (Everything) [24] and DR 15 (Fire) [15] - the body's inhuman resilience
  • Monstrous [-25] - the appearance under the glamour that produces a -5 reaction if seen

Secret (Dragonborn under glamour) [-20]: the true nature is hidden. Without the amulet, the room reacts at -5. Discovery would end his operational mobility entirely.

Secret Identity (Siegfried Vane's human face is a glamour) [-10]: the public identity is itself a layer of concealment - not merely hidden gifts but a hidden species.

Required Secret Society

His kind. Siegfried is a Dragonborn operating behind a glamour in a world that does not acknowledge his kind exist. Others of his kind who similarly conceal themselves constitute the hidden community — an informal society of concealment rather than a named institutional order.

Secret (Dragonborn under glamour) [-20] serves as both the personal secret and the society tie. The glamour is the membership card. A formal Patron advantage is not required. FRH does not count toward this requirement.

How Siegfried Uses His Powers

  • His fire spell suite - Fireball-19, Explosive Fireball-19, Create Fire-19, Shape Fire-19, Ignite Fire-19, Heat-19 - makes him the most capable offensive magical combatant in any party he joins. At skill 19 these are not situational options; they are reliable weapons.
  • His illusion and concealment suite - Perfect Illusion-19, Complex Illusion-19, Invisibility-19, Blur-19, Darkness-19 - gives him the full range of tactical concealment options for party operations.
  • His truth and detection suite - Truthsayer-19, Detect Magic-19, Mage Sight-19, See Invisible-19, Sense Emotion-19, Sense Foes-19 - covers investigation and threat-assessment at high competence.
  • The Dragon Amulet creates a permanent tactical vulnerability that defines his operational behavior. He cannot afford the amulet to be broken, stolen, or suppressed. This shapes what risks he takes and what environments he enters.
  • His Eidetic Memory 2 [60] means he retains everything he has seen or read with unusual precision - compounding his investigative capability beyond the magical lane.

Why He Works

Siegfried proves that a Dragonborn in 1923 reads best when the concealment cost is load-bearing. His pride, discipline, and control are not ornamental - they are what it costs to remain legible in a world that would strip him of operational identity the moment the glamour slips.

Character Overview

Siegfried Vane lives under a glamour because the world he moves through has never offered him a safer alternative. Beneath that mask is a body no polite city would know how to tolerate calmly: scales with a bronze-red cast, ember-bright eyes, a horned brow, and the unmistakable fact of blood that was never meant to pass for ordinary humanity. Yet concealment has never made him ordinary in spirit. Even hidden, he carries himself with the dangerous self-command of someone who knows exactly how much force he keeps leashed, and exactly what it costs to remain legible inside a society built for smaller truths.

That tension is what makes him more than a dragonborn analogue dropped unchanged into another setting. Siegfried belongs in FRH because the world has to answer what draconic inheritance means when it appears under urban smoke, lies, glamour, and investigation instead of open myth. He is proud, vigilant, and civilized in the hard sense of the word. He did not become disciplined because life was gentle. He became disciplined because without discipline he would have been reduced to spectacle, hunted out of the rooms he needed, or surrendered to the hotter, simpler interpretation other people would happily impose on him.

His power therefore reads in layers. There is the physical truth of the body, the sorcerous truth of old fire and command, and the social truth of glamour as survival. Siegfried is not merely a bruiser with scales and not merely a stylish occult aristocrat. He is a man who has had to decide, repeatedly, whether pride will be armor, burden, or vice. In party terms he widens the world: ancient lineages, hidden schools, rival traditions, long roads, ports, estates, and older forms of nonhuman pressure all become more real around him. He can make FRH feel broader, stranger, and more mythically distributed than its most civic and bureaucratic lanes.

For a new companion, Siegfried should read as someone who became controlled because the alternative was catastrophe. He is not asking the world to forgive or celebrate what he is. He is asking it to endure the fact that he exists, that he sees clearly, and that if the mask slips the room will have to reckon with more than one kind of fire.
