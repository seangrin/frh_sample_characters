NPC Companion - Faerie Guardian. Chuff is Melody Manners's Ally advantage made physical. He is not a player character and not an FRH operative. He appears on Melody's 15-or-less Ally roll. His stats are presented here for reference and encounter use.

Chuff

469-point faerie guardian with the mass of a lion, the presence of a shrine sentinel, and the composure of something that understands people more than it should. DR 14, Invisibility, Insubstantiality, Super Running 65 mph, Sonic Blast (sonic 3d, Area Effect, Range 30), Claws (cut 6d+1). Voice is a harmony of woman singing.

Powers

  • DR 14 (Everything) [42]: conventional 1923-era firearms land on DR 14 before any damage reaches him. Pistols, rifles, and shotguns are not effective at range against Chuff. Combined with Extra Hit Points +10 and HT 15, he is extraordinarily difficult to put down.
  • Invisibility [48] (Carry Objects: Light Encumbrance +20%): can become invisible while carrying up to Light Encumbrance. In practice, this means he can shadow Melody invisibly, move through crowds undetected, and enter hostile environments without detection. The enemy-side implication: no one who doesn't know Chuff exists can defend against him.
  • Insubstantiality [96] (Carry Objects: Light Encumbrance +20%): can pass through walls, floors, and closed environments while remaining capable of carrying Melody at Light Encumbrance. This extends his surveillance reach into any space she cannot enter physically.
  • Super Running 2 [40] - 31.625 mph / Move 9: extraordinary ground speed. In a 1923 context where automobiles average 20-40 mph on good roads, Chuff can run down most ground vehicles in open terrain.
  • Super Jump 2 [20]: High Jump 32 feet, Broad Jump 43 yards. Vertical and horizontal movement obstacles are operationally irrelevant.
  • Sonic Blast 14(3) - sonic 3d, Area Effect, Range 30 [44]: affects substantial targets at range 30 with area effect. Sonic damage bypasses armor that blocks physical attacks. This is his only true ranged offensive capability - but it is decisive at the skill level and area it covers.
  • Claws (Talons) - imp 4d-1 / cut 6d+1 [40] and Sharp Teeth - cut 4d-1 [5]: close-combat output that turns his ST 35 into catastrophic per-hit damage against unarmored targets. Against DR 10 or less, a claw hit is typically decisive.
  • Enemy [-120] (15 or less, ×3): the largest disadvantage on his sheet and the most dangerous fact of his situation. An Enemy appearing on 15 or less is nearly certain to appear; ×3 modifier indicates multiple enemies or extraordinary enemy power. Whatever hunts Chuff (and through him, Melody) is persistent, powerful, and nearly always present.

Relationship to Melody

Ally (Faerie Lion - Chuff) (451-500 points, 15 or less) [135] appears on Melody's sheet. Chuff is that advantage given physical form.

Special Rapport [10] is present on both sheets. The rapport is mutual and directional - it is not generic guardian behavior but a specific bond between Chuff and Melody that defines his loyalty and orientation.

Duty (Protect Melody) (15 or less) [-15]: his only formal obligation beyond his own survival. He does not serve FRH, the party, or any abstract principle. He protects Melody.

Shared disadvantages - Staurophobia (Mild) and Vulnerability 1 (Pure Iron) - appear on both sheets. The faerie vulnerability is not Melody's alone; Chuff shares it completely.

Concealment and Discovery

Secret (Supernatural Creature) [-30]: the largest single secret in the campaign pair. Chuff is not a large dog, not a trained animal, and not explicable by 1923-era naturalism. Discovery of his existence ends Melody's ability to operate in any normal social context.

His Invisibility and Insubstantiality mean he can remain near Melody in almost any environment without detection. The operational question is not whether he can stay hidden - he can - but whether the Enemy he carries (appearing on 15 or less) eventually creates a situation where hiding is no longer possible.

The Unnatural Feature (Voice is a Harmony of Woman Singing) [-5] is the tell that cannot be suppressed when he speaks. He speaks Gaelic natively. When his voice is heard in the wrong context, the concealment fails regardless of his physical location.

No FRH Package

Chuff carries no FRH Patron, no FRH Duty, and no FRH Enemy. He is not an FRH operative and was not recruited by the Followers of the Right Hand. His only institutional obligation is Duty (Protect Melody).

His Enemy [-120] is his own - separate from the FRH enemy structure that bears on Melody. The campaign is therefore running two distinct threat tracks on this pair: FRH enemies (targeting Melody through her operative role) and Chuff's personal enemies (targeting him, and through him, her).

There is no Required Secret Society for Chuff. He belongs to no organization. His society is the faerie world accessed through blood and bond, not through initiation.

Tactical Notes

  • Default engagement: invisible, insubstantial shadow accompanying Melody. Transitions to physical when a threat exceeds her capacity to manage it alone.
  • Sonic Blast at Range 30 with Area Effect allows him to engage multiple targets at distance while still invisible - if the sonic origin point is not tracked, Chuff's position remains hidden after the blast.
  • In close combat, ST 35 with Talons (cut 6d+1) and skill Brawling-17 means most 1923-era combatants do not survive extended contact. The limiting factor is not his combat capability - it is the concealment cost of using it.
  • Frightens Animals [-5] makes animal handling impossible near him. In 1923 contexts where horses, dogs, and other animals are common, his presence triggers animal responses that can compromise the cover Melody is maintaining.
  • The Enemy appearing on 15 or less means the GM should treat Chuff's enemy as present in nearly every session. It is not a background fact; it is a load-bearing campaign pressure on the Melody/Chuff pair.
  • Horizontal [-10] and No Fine Manipulators [-30] mean Chuff cannot use human tools, open most doors physically, or interact with 1923-era material objects in conventional ways. He operates through presence, force, movement, and Melody.

Why He Works

Chuff works because he is the visible proof that Melody is not metaphorical. His 469 points are not decoration - they are the threat backing every social interaction she has. The Enemy [-120] is what keeps him from being a guaranteed safe resource: he is the thing that draws Melody's most dangerous enemies toward her life, and the thing that keeps those enemies from winning. The pair are structurally inseparable because that is what the point architecture actually says.

Character Overview

Chuff is not a pet, mascot, or convenient fantasy mount. He is the visible proof that Melody Manners lives too close to the faerie world for ordinary explanations to survive contact with her. Built like a shrine guardian translated into living muscle, fur, antler, and watchfulness, he exists at the point where protection and unease become the same emotion. People are meant to look at him and understand, before anyone speaks, that whatever follows Melody is not metaphorical, theatrical, or safely domestic.

His loyalty is real, but it is not the loyalty of a trained animal. Chuff protects Melody with the total seriousness of a being who appears to understand that she is his charge, his companion, and perhaps the condition under which he can remain properly anchored in the human world at all. He can hide, move, and endure like something built for impossible thresholds. His speed, silence, and predatory force make him terrifying when violence begins, but the stranger truth is that even at rest he alters the moral temperature of a room. He makes secrecy feel guarded, glamour feel dangerous, and every social scene around Melody feel as if it already has claws waiting just offstage.

For reader purposes, Chuff works best as Melody's inhuman counterpart rather than as an isolated creature profile. He is the faerie pressure inside her story made visible: beautiful in a way that is not safe, protective in a way that is not gentle, and supernatural in a way that refuses to stay symbolic. If Melody is the part of the pair that opens doors, smiles past suspicion, and makes the human world porous, Chuff is the ancient answer standing behind that opening, ensuring that what crosses through will not be mistaken for anything ordinary.
