Core Profile

Amber learned to pay careful attention before she understood what she was paying attention to. As a half-Chinese woman navigating 1923 America - with the Chinese Exclusion Act still active, with the assumptions made about her arriving in every room before she did - watchfulness was a condition of survival long before it was a gift. What she noticed over time was that some of what she saw did not have ordinary explanations. People in pain pulled at her in ways that went beyond empathy. Fear arrived in spaces before anyone had spoken it. The moral and spiritual state of strangers turned out to be legible to her in ways she could not account for on any terms she had been given. The shaded glasses she has worn since her training years are partly about managing how much of that comes in at once.

The Lotus Bell found her, trained her, and gave language to what she already was. In China she learned medicine, herbs, self-command, and the careful use of psionic and magical gifts that were never meant to be displayed carelessly in a world that denied their existence. What the training also gave her was obligation: a secret society, enemies who actively hunt Lotus Bell practitioners, and a permanent requirement for secrecy that she is constitutionally poor at maintaining. Amber cannot lie. Her conscience is legible to anyone paying attention. She is a woman trained in hidden arts and bound to a covert organization, carrying a moral transparency that makes strategic deception almost physically uncomfortable - not as a failing but as a load-bearing fact about who she is. She chose to stay anyway. That choice has never fully resolved.

Her power runs in three directions at once: she senses, she heals, and she reads. Psionic Healing is her apex - she can close wounds that have run out of ordinary options, stabilize bodies that should not be stabilizable. Her ESP and Telepathy give her Sense Aura, Clairvoyance, Precognition, and Emotion Sense. She reads spiritual states and registers the moral weight of spaces before most people have finished walking through the door. Magery 1 adds a practical spell shelf oriented toward sensing, protection, and supplementary healing. Combined, she functions as the party's diagnostic layer: she does not just treat what went wrong, she can often name what caused it, and she is genuinely difficult to deceive about the nature of a situation even when deception would be in everyone's interest.

In the field she is the party's healer and its most sensitive early-warning system, with a Judo ground game and a complete weapons familiarity that suggests the Lotus Bell's training philosophy was not purely meditative. She moves conservatively, speaks carefully, and notices everything - which means she will notice what the party is hiding from itself as readily as she notices what an antagonist is hiding from the party. She is bound by conscience to intervene when someone is hurt, bound by pacifism to avoid harming innocents, and bound by truthfulness to say what she has actually seen. In a world full of people who survive by managing what they reveal, Amber is the member of the party who already knows what everyone is carrying, and who cannot pretend otherwise.
