Description

Ruth Calder learned the dead from the bottom up. Before she ever became a discreet inquiry agent, she had already lived in the bureaucratic half-light of morgues, records, and the city"™s quieter embarrassments. She could move from a society-page assignment to a morgue drawer without losing her composure, which made her useful in Los Angeles in exactly the wrong way. She learned who was lying for status, who was lying from fear, and who was lying because a corpse or a scandal was about to become expensive. That combination of public polish and private curiosity was what put her too close to the Del Rey morgue fire while chasing a corruption story, and that fire is the event around which the rest of her life still burns.

After Del Rey, Ruth came away altered. The dead and the objects around them no longer stayed silent for her. Psychic residue clung to evidence. Danger announced itself early enough to make her flinch. Scenes, bodies, and belongings began to carry aftermath in ways she could feel before she could always explain them. The result did not turn her into a spectacular psychic. It turned her into something more useful and more exhausting: an investigator who can read evidence the city itself would prefer to leave unspoken. She keeps going because once you know the dead are still pressing on the world, it becomes difficult to treat their cases as routine.

Ruth"™s public self remains tailored, composed, and socially functional. She can still work rooms, ask questions, and pass as someone only a little too interested in other people"™s damage. Private life is harder. She sleeps poorly, drinks more than she should, and carries smoke, residue, and memory around inside her in a way that no amount of polish quite defeats. Her social gift is not false; it is simply no longer innocent. She has seen too much administrative death and too many civic lies to mistake refinement for goodness.

For a new player, Ruth should feel polished in public and sleepless in private. She is a Los Angeles inquiry agent built from morgue work, corruption stories, and one fire too many. Her powers are not there to make her flashy. They are there to make every object, every room, and every dead person potentially refuse to stay quiet in her presence.
