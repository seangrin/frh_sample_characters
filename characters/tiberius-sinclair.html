Tiberius Sinclair

Filthy-rich industrial heir and laudanum-dependent telepathic vigilante - "The Wraith" - whose psychic gifts made ordinary life impossible and whose double life was the only answer he found.

Powers

  • Telepathy 6 [36] (Instantaneous: +20%): the full operational psionic suite - Telereceive-17, Telescan-16, Telesend-15, Telecontrol-14, Suggest-17, Sleep-15, Mental Blow-15, Mental Stab-15, Mindsword-15, Mind Shield-16, Emotion Sense-14, Psi Sense-15, Erase Signature-14, Signature Sniffer-14. He can read, influence, suppress, or attack minds at range. This is not subtle - it is a complete telepathic assault and investigation toolkit.
  • Telekinesis 5 [20]: fine-manipulation telekinesis that can move objects up to ~4 oz. at 4 yd/sec. At this power level it is investigative and operative rather than a combat tool - lock manipulation, evidence retrieval, silent interference.
  • ESP 5 (no skill): powers Danger Sense. He senses threats before they materialize.
  • Filthy Rich [50] ($75,000 starting wealth): the public infrastructure that makes the Wraith identity operationally possible. The wealth is not flavor - it funds the equipment, the identity, and the access.

Secret (Psionic powers) [-30]: the full scope of his telepathic and TK capabilities is hidden. Discovery would collapse his operational identity, his social position, and his Wraith cover simultaneously.

Secret Identity (The Wraith) [-10]: the double life itself is a compound secret - both the identity and the nature of his nighttime operations must be concealed.

Patron (Wraith Society) (9 or less) [5]: Secret: -5. This is the concealed organizational lane that supports the Wraith identity. FRH does not count toward this requirement.

How Tiberius Uses His Powers

  • Telereceive-17 lets him read minds directly at short range - motive, memory, hidden agenda, and real-time fear are available to him in a way that no conventional interview technique can replicate.
  • Telescan-16 lets him sweep an area for psionic signatures, living presence, and mental activity before physical entry - effectively a telepathic advance scout capability.
  • Suggest-17 and Sleep-15 give him non-violent options for neutralizing or redirecting threats and witnesses without leaving evidence.
  • Telekinesis at Power 5 handles fine-manipulation tasks: lock mechanisms, document retrieval, silent interference with evidence, and operator tools that require a finger-width of manipulation at distance.
  • His Disguise-15, Stealth-15, Holdout-15, and extensive underworld / high-society skill set make him a capable field operative even without deploying the psionic layer.
  • His Addiction (Laudanum) is a real operational constraint. It creates incapacitation risk in extended sequences, creates social exposure when managing supply, and shapes the chemistry of every scene where he is under sustained pressure.

Why He Works

Tiberius proves that wealth and psychic damage go together in FRH the way they do in the real world - the resources that make the double life possible are also part of what makes the gifts unbearable without chemical management. The laudanum is not flavor. It is the price of the volume being turned up too high on reality.

Character Overview

Tiberius Sinclair was born into the kind of wealth that teaches a man very early how many doors will open for him before he has earned any of them. The Sinclair family sits atop an industrial empire broad enough to warp whole regions of the American West, and from the outside Tiberius looks exactly like the sort of heir that system was designed to produce: handsome, polished, expensive, and socially effortless. He learned formal manners, private confidence, and how to move through moneyed rooms as if they were his natural climate. What those rooms never taught him was peace. Even while he was still young, the world was already pressing too hard on him from the inside.

His psychic gifts made ordinary life impossible in ways other people could not see. Strain, pressure, motive, and hidden intention stopped remaining abstract. Distance, privacy, and social surfaces became unreliable. Tiberius could charm a ballroom and still leave feeling skinned raw by everything passing through it. That is part of why his double life took shape. By day he remained the rakish socialite the family name could comfortably display. By night he became the Wraith, a clandestine operator using telepathy, telekinesis, and cultivated nerve to act where ordinary society had no language for what was wrong. The mask is not merely costume. It is a survival structure built out of secrecy, privilege, and necessity.

What makes Tiberius valuable in FRH is that he binds together several pressures the setting likes to keep in tension: high society and occult damage, wealth and secrecy, heroism and self-destruction. He is not an uncomplicated vigilante and not a carefree pulp ace. He carries addiction, nightmares, hidden powers, and the corrosive knowledge that his gifts have made him useful in ways his public world could never safely acknowledge. The laudanum matters because it is not flavor. It is one of the prices he pays for continuing to live with the volume turned up too high on reality.

To a new companion, Tiberius should read as a man who was given every visible advantage except the ability to remain untouched by what he could feel and what he eventually learned. He is glamorous, yes, but the glamour is effort. Beneath it is a psychic investigator, a damaged heir, and a man trying very hard to remain himself before the night, the secrets, or the chemicals decide to define him first.
