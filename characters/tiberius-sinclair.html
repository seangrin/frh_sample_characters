Description

Tiberius Sinclair was born into the kind of wealth that teaches a man very early how many doors will open for him before he has earned any of them. The Sinclair family sits atop an industrial empire broad enough to warp whole regions of the American West, and from the outside Tiberius looks exactly like the sort of heir that system was designed to produce: handsome, polished, expensive, and socially effortless. He learned formal manners, private confidence, and how to move through moneyed rooms as if they were his natural climate. What those rooms never taught him was peace. Even while he was still young, the world was already pressing too hard on him from the inside.

His psychic gifts made ordinary life impossible in ways other people could not see. Strain, pressure, motive, and hidden intention stopped remaining abstract. Distance, privacy, and social surfaces became unreliable. Tiberius could charm a ballroom and still leave feeling skinned raw by everything passing through it. That is part of why his double life took shape. By day he remained the rakish socialite the family name could comfortably display. By night he became the Wraith, a clandestine operator using telepathy, telekinesis, and cultivated nerve to act where ordinary society had no language for what was wrong. The mask is not merely costume. It is a survival structure built out of secrecy, privilege, and necessity.

What makes Tiberius valuable in FRH is that he binds together several pressures the setting likes to keep in tension: high society and occult damage, wealth and secrecy, heroism and self-destruction. He is not an uncomplicated vigilante and not a carefree pulp ace. He carries addiction, nightmares, hidden powers, and the corrosive knowledge that his gifts have made him useful in ways his public world could never safely acknowledge. The laudanum matters because it is not flavor. It is one of the prices he pays for continuing to live with the volume turned up too high on reality.

To a new companion, Tiberius should read as a man who was given every visible advantage except the ability to remain untouched by what he could feel and what he eventually learned. He is glamorous, yes, but the glamour is effort. Beneath it is a psychic investigator, a damaged heir, and a man trying very hard to remain himself before the night, the secrets, or the chemicals decide to define him first.
