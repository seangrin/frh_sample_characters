Autafon "Amber" Deliamber

Psi Build

The build where pure psionic depth runs deepest - IQ 18, Telepathy 6 (upgraded from 5), Astral Projection 3, and the full sensing suite operating at its highest skill levels: Detect Lies-19, Psychology-19, Clairvoyance-17, Sense Aura-17.

Powers - Psi Build

  • Astral Projection 3 [9] - Astral Projection-16, Astral Sight-16: the distinguishing capability of this build. The other two configurations have no Astral Projection at all. At power level 3 with skill 16, Amber can project consciousness outside her body - scouting, sensing, and operating in spaces her physical form cannot safely enter. The two-form capacity (physical body / projected self) is a unique operational resource available only in this build.
  • Telepathy 6 [15] - upgraded from Telepathy 5 in the other two builds: the additional power level supports higher-tier telepathic capability. In this build Emotion Sense reaches skill 16 and Detect Lies reaches 19 - both higher than the Dragon Breath and Martial Artist configurations.
  • ESP 5 [15]: Clairaudience-17, Clairvoyance-17, Precognition-17, Sense Aura-17. All sensing skills are at their ceiling in this build because IQ 18 raises every IQ-based skill floor. This is Amber's perceptive suite at maximum depth.
  • Psionic Healing 15 [45] - Psionic Healing-18: the healing core is constant across all three builds. This build's higher IQ does not change the healing skill - Psionic Healing-18 appears in all three configurations - but every other IQ-based skill is elevated.
  • IQ 18 [125] - the build's defining investment: Psychology-19, Detect Lies-19, Streetwise-17, Research-17, Merchant-16, Hypnotism-16. Every professional and investigative skill operates from a higher base. The cost of IQ 18 vs IQ 17 is what removes Dragon Breath and reduces DX from 17 to 16.

How This Build's Powers Are Used

  • Astral Projection-16 allows Amber to send her consciousness ahead of the party - through a locked door, into a guarded room, or into a space that would be physically dangerous to enter. Her body remains in place while her projected self operates in the target space with Astral Sight-16. In a 1923 investigation context, this is an intelligence-gathering capability with no physical equivalent.
  • Detect Lies-19 at this skill level is effectively a lie detector in conversation. Telepathy 6 and IQ 18 together mean she reads people at a depth the other two builds cannot match. Psychology-19 adds analytic framing to the raw sensing. Combined, she can assess witness reliability, interrogate under cover of ordinary conversation, and identify deception almost automatically.
  • The full ESP suite - Clairvoyance-17, Clairaudience-17, Precognition-17, Sense Aura-17 - operates at its highest effective skill level in this build. IQ 18 means every ESP skill sits two points above the Dragon Breath and Martial Artist equivalents. The sensing reach is the same; the reliability is higher.
  • Psionic Healing-18 operates identically to the other builds - touch-range healing at high skill. The Psi build does not improve healing beyond the common ceiling; it expands what she can do between encounters.
  • In close combat, this build has Judo-17 (Parry 12) and Karate-15 - the same or slightly weaker than the other configurations. DX 16 vs 17 lowers every combat skill by one point relative to the Dragon Breath and Martial Artist builds. She is not helpless in contact range, but the combat depth belongs to the other two.
  • Pacifism (Cannot Harm Innocents) constrains all psionic and physical options. Astral Projection does not grant license to spy without cause - she will not use it against noncombatants any more than she would use Pressure Points.

Character Overview

Autafon "Amber" Deliamber learned early that being visibly decent does not make the world gentle. The Lotus Bell did not make her into something she had never been - it gave discipline, language, and obligation to gifts that were already pushing at the edges of her life. In the Psi build, that depth runs inward rather than outward: no dragon fire, no cinematic striking suite - instead, IQ 18, Astral Projection, and a psionic sensing apparatus that operates at its highest effective ceiling across every skill in the cluster.

What the Psi build makes available that no other configuration offers is the out-of-body option. Astral Projection at skill 16 means Amber can send her consciousness ahead of the party - into the locked room, down the hallway, into the space where the body would be immediately recognized or immediately endangered. Her physical form remains behind, inert and vulnerable, while her projected self operates. In a 1923 investigation, this is often the difference between knowing what is in the next room before the door opens and not.

The IQ 18 investment raises every professional and investigative skill in the sheet. Detect Lies-19 and Psychology-19 together make her the sharpest reader of human motivation in any configuration. She does not just sense that someone is lying - she models why, from what position, toward what end. In an FRH investigation where almost everyone is hiding something, this is the single most useful passive capability she carries.

What makes Amber consistent across all three builds is everything this build shares with the others: Psionic Healing-18, the full ESP/Telepathy sensing suite, Pacifism (Cannot Harm Innocents), the Lotus Bell obligation, the conservative dress and shaded glasses managing the tell she cannot suppress. The configuration changes what she brings to a crisis. The person underneath does not change at all.
