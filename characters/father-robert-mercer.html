Powers

  • Magery 3 [35]: Robert is a genuine high-power magical practitioner. His spell list is heavily oriented toward healing, spirit-facing, truth, protection, and detection - consistent with a pastoral rather than aggressive magic use model.
  • True Faith [15] and Blessed 1 [10]: spiritual authority that compounds his magical capacity with genuine faith-based access. His Exorcism-25 is built on both.
  • Spirit and truth suite: Sense Spirit-22, Sense Foes-20, Truthsayer-22, Turn Spirit-26, and Affect Spirits-20 make him an extraordinarily capable operator against the supernatural layer of any case.
  • Healing: Minor Healing-26 and Lend Health-22 mean he provides real medical-adjacent support. He carries a Powerstone (5) and a firearm as a last-resort tool, not a primary one.

Secret (Magery and occult ministry) [-20]: the full scope of his magical ability is hidden from his Church superiors and from the public. A priest with Magery 3 in 1923 America is not merely unusual - he is institutionally dangerous to acknowledge.

Required Secret Society

The church-basement labor organizing network. Robert runs a covert grassroots infrastructure through his parish basement - a hidden network connecting workers, labor reporters, settlement house nurses, and others who cannot operate openly.

Secret (Church-basement labor organizing network) [-20].

Note: The source sheet does not list a formal Patron advantage for this network. The secret society tie is expressed entirely through the Secret disadvantage and the contact/reputation cluster. This appears to be a deliberate design choice - the network is something Robert built and runs rather than something he was inducted into. If a formal named organization should be associated, this would need human review.

FRH does not satisfy the secret-society requirement.

How Father Mercer Uses His Powers

  • His spell suite turns him into the most capable spirit-facing caster in any party. Turn Spirit-26 and Affect Spirits-20 at those skill levels are operationally decisive against supernatural presences.
  • Truthsayer-22 lets him run verification on confessions, interrogations, and negotiations with a precision no mundane Detect Lies skill matches.
  • His healing suite - Minor Healing-26, Lend Health-22, Lend Strength-17 - covers real physical damage. He is the closest thing the party has to a genuine battlefield medic with additional options beyond anatomy.
  • His Exorcism-25 backed by Magery 3 and True Faith makes him the most capable exorcist in the library. He is not cautious about it the way Father Kane is - he has the power, and he knows it.
  • His labor network gives him neighborhood-level intelligence no other character can match. He knows who is hungry, who is scared, who is hiding, and who in the parish has encountered something that cannot be explained by any natural cause.

Why He Works

Robert proves that a parish priest with Magery 3 reads best when the magic is fully integrated with his vocation - not a genre intrusion but a deepening of who he already was. His faith is not theatrical. It is organized, practical, and stubborn enough to keep the supernatural honest.

Character Overview

Father Robert Mercer belongs to the part of a city that polite society likes to call ordinary while living off the labor it never quite sees. He is a Chicago parish priest whose days run through relief kitchens, hospital visits, funerals, labor disputes, confessions, and the endless neighborhood work of keeping people from going under one at a time. He is broad-shouldered, careful, and calm in the way that makes frightened people hand him the truth without noticing exactly when they decided to trust him. Wealth and power tend to underestimate that gentleness until they discover that it covers a much harder commitment to worker dignity, mutual aid, and moral accountability than they had planned to accommodate.

Robert's priesthood is therefore not decorative and not detached. It is municipal, bodily, and social. He knows who is hungry, who is striking, who has lost a child, who is trying to bury someone cheaply, who is lying about a wound, and which family is too proud to admit it needs help. The hidden labor network in his church basement is not an ideological hobby; it is an extension of his sacramental life. He believes that a priest should know what rents are doing, what employers are doing, and who in the parish is being quietly broken by both. That same seriousness shapes his occult life. If the supernatural touches his world, it touches kitchens, wards, boarding houses, picket lines, and funeral parlors before it touches any grand cathedral abstraction.

His magic grew out of that life rather than interrupting it. Robert was a priest first and only then a serious caster. The gift sharpened what was already there: healing, discernment, truth, spirit-sense, protection, and a capacity to stand in bad rooms without yielding them immediately to fear. He does not feel like a fantasy cleric dropped into 1923. He feels like a real parish priest whose vocation proved deep enough to carry impossible responsibility once it arrived. That is why he can steady a frightened family, read a hospital ward, confront a lie without cruelty, and still call on powers that most of his own Church would rather not have to explain.

For a new player, Robert should feel warm to the people who need him and immovable to the people who try to use them. He looks gentle until he decides not to yield. His faith is not theatrical. It is organized, practical, neighborhood-bound, and stubborn enough to keep one hand on the soup line and the other on the edge of the occult when the city requires both.
