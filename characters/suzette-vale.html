Suzette Vale

Vampire mortuary consultant who does not need fangs to be unnerving - death-world professional first, disciplined predator second, and terrifying because she knows the right rite before anyone has decided which truth they were hoping to conceal.

Powers

  • Vampiric Immortality [60]: she does not age in the conventional sense and has been operating in death-world professional circles long enough to have accumulated a depth of contact, reputation, and institutional knowledge no living practitioner could match.
  • Vampiric Invulnerability [150]: extreme damage resistance that makes ordinary injury operationally irrelevant. Specific vulnerabilities (see Disadvantages) constrain her operating environment rather than her general capability.
  • Vampiric Dominance 2 [35]: the predator layer. Used with discretion. She is not a brute and not theatrical about it.
  • Magery 1 [15] with spirit and dominance spells: Beast Summoning (Mammals), Body of Air, Charm, Mammal Control - a narrow but precise magical toolkit consistent with her operational requirements.
  • Eidetic Memory 2 [60] and nine languages: she remembers everything and can move through every major cultural context that touches the New Orleans / New York death-world geography.

Required Secret Power

Vampire nature (grouped):

  • Vampiric Immortality [60] - she does not age
  • Vampiric Invulnerability [150] - exceptional damage resistance
  • Vampiric Dominance 2 [35] - predatory influence capacity
  • Bite [30] - physical feeding mechanism
  • Dark Vision [25] - sensory enhancement

Secret (Vampire under the Masquerade) [-30]: her true nature is concealed under professional identity. Disclosure would end her operational access and invite supernatural and institutional targeting simultaneously.

Required Secret Society

Patron (Kindred Compact) (9 or less) [10].

The Kindred Compact is Suzette's non-FRH hidden-order lane - the vampiric organizational structure that bounds her politically and governs the Masquerade she operates under. FRH does not count toward this requirement.

Secret (Patron (Kindred Compact)) [-20].

The Compact's existence and her membership are concealed. The discipline of the Masquerade is not personal preference - it is the operating framework the Compact enforces and she is embedded within.

How Suzette Uses Her Powers

  • She is the party's specialist in death-world institutional access - funeral homes, viewing rooms, morgues, chapel basements, cemetery offices, and the quiet professional networks that surround difficult bodies and contested burials.
  • Her nine languages let her navigate every cultural community that touches the death-world geography of New Orleans and New York City without dependency on translators or intermediaries.
  • Vampiric Dominance 2 gives her the capacity to compel or suppress cooperation when professional persuasion is not enough, but her design emphasizes discretion over spectacle. She does not lead with the predator mode.
  • Body of Air and Charm give her infiltration and social manipulation options beyond the mundane. Mammal Control lets her move information through environments where human agents draw attention.
  • Her operational vulnerabilities - Dependency (blood), Dependency (native soil), the three Dreads, No Shadow, No Reflection, Nocturnal, Cannot Enter Without Invitation - create genuine geographic and temporal constraints that require advance planning and party support to manage.

Why She Works

Suzette proves that a vampire reads best in FRH when the supernatural layer disciplines rather than replaces the professional identity. She was already useful before she was undead. The Masquerade has made her more exact, not less human in the places that actually matter to investigation.

Character Overview

Suzette Vale was born to the mourning trade in the way some women are born to music or law: so thoroughly suited to its manners and emotional temperatures that even the name seems to have arrived carrying funeral flowers with it. In New Orleans she built a life around the hidden administration of death. She knows funeral homes, viewing rooms, chapel basements, clergy conferences, cemetery offices, morgue procedures, difficult bodies, and the minor lies families tell when grief meets ritual obligation. Undertakers, embalmers, cemetery staff, priests, and medical examiners consult her quietly when something is obscure, delicate, or wrong. She does not need fangs to be unnerving. It is enough that she can stand beside a body and know whether the family, the rite, or the corpse itself is refusing the truth.

Vampirism did not erase that professional identity. It disciplined it into a darker and more exact form. Suzette is a real vampire, but not a brute and not a theatrical one. She is useful, discreet, politically bounded by a narrow Kindred Compact, and deeply embedded in death-work to the point that removing her would inconvenience too many of the wrong people. What makes her dangerous is not frenzy but composure. She is broad in knowledge, multilingual in a city that demands it, and terrifying in the particular way of someone who understands that grief, ritual, and the handling of the dead all create opportunities for lies no less than for tenderness.

That is why she works so well as a guest investigator, consultant, or pressure figure. Suzette changes the room the moment she enters it. She belongs to ledgers of death, inherited custom, difficult burials, obscure rites, and those private conferences where the living try to decide what they owe the dead without admitting what the dead may already know. Her monstrosity does not sit beside her profession as an ironic contrast. It has become one more way in which she is at home among people, records, and bodies that most others can only approach temporarily.

For a new player, Suzette should feel like a death-world professional first and a disciplined predator second. She is elegant, exact, and useful in places where sentiment is not enough. If she frightens people, it is often because she knows the right rite, the right body-truth, and the right family lie before anyone else at the table has decided which of those things they were hoping to conceal.
