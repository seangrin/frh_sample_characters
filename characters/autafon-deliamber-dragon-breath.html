Autafon "Amber" Deliamber

Dragon Breath Build

The build that pairs the Lotus Bell's healing and sensing gifts with a supernatural ranged weapon - Dragon's Breath 16(5), fire 5d at range 50, three times a day - and the highest DX and gun skill of the three configurations.

Powers - Dragon Breath Build

  • Dragon's Breath 16(5) - fire 5d, Range 50, 3/day [31]: the distinguishing feature of this build. Instantaneous (+20%), Full Power Only (-10%), Limited Use 3/day (-20%). At range 50, she can engage from well outside melee contact. At fire 5d, the damage is comparable to Silas Brent's Hellfire Shot in the 1870 party - a decisive ranged supernatural weapon in a 1923-era context where most combatants have no magical protection.
  • ESP 5 [15] and Telepathy 5 [10]: the psionic sensing and communication foundation - Clairaudience-16, Clairvoyance-16, Precognition-16, Emotion Sense-15, Sense Aura-16, Detect Lies-18. She reads the emotional and psychic field of every room she enters.
  • Psionic Healing 15 [45]: Psionic Healing-18. The healing capability is constant across all three builds - this is Amber's anchor role regardless of which configuration is in play.
  • DX 17 [100] and Guns (Pistol)-19 [1]: in this build the physical capability is at maximum. Judo-17 and Knife-16 back up the ranged option in close combat. Guns-19 is the highest of the three builds.
  • Unnatural Feature (Eyes Glow Green): the Dragon Breath manifestation has a visible tell. The eyes are part of it.

Required Secret Power

Psionic gifts and Dragon's Breath (grouped):

  • ESP 5 [15] - clairvoyance, precognition, aura sensing
  • Telepathy 5 [10] - emotion sense, detect lies at psionic depth

No explicit Secret disadvantage for the psionic cluster appears beyond the Lotus Bell membership secret. The Dragon's Breath is almost certainly concealed - it appears to be covered under the Lotus Bell secrecy obligation rather than a separate named secret.

Required Secret Society

The Lotus Bell.

Secret (Member of The Lotus Bell) [-20] is present. The Lotus Bell is the non-FRH hidden order - a real operating organization given that it has its own dedicated Enemy (Hunter of Lotus Bells).

Note: No formal Patron advantage for the Lotus Bell appears in the source. The affiliation is present as a Secret disadvantage but not as a Patron. This may reflect the Lotus Bell providing training and obligation without ongoing material support - or a source gap. The current page preserves the source as written.

FRH does not satisfy the secret-society requirement.

How This Build's Powers Are Used

  • Dragon's Breath at Range 50 means Amber can engage from well outside standard melee distance in a 1923 environment. Three uses per day makes it a decisive escalation option rather than a default - she reserves it for situations where conventional approaches have failed or the threat requires it.
  • Guns (Pistol)-19 backed by DX 17 makes this build an unusually capable shooter by conventional standards as well. She is not a pure support character - she can deliver fire damage at range whether the Dragon's Breath is available or not.
  • Psionic Healing-18 functions the same as in all three builds: touch-range healing at high skill. In the Dragon Breath build she can move from offensive fire to field medicine within the same scene.
  • The ESP/Telepathy cluster - Detect Lies-18, Emotion Sense-15, Clairvoyance-16, Sense Aura-16, Precognition-16 - operates identically across all three builds. She reads rooms, people, danger, and hidden states before anyone has spoken.
  • Judo-17 and Karate-15 provide close-combat backup if neither the Dragon's Breath nor the pistol is appropriate. Knife Throwing-17 adds a thrown weapon option.
  • The Unnatural Feature (Eyes Glow Green) is a disclosure risk whenever the Dragon Breath or intense psionic activity is active. The conservative dress, hair-up habit, and shaded glasses are all management strategies for a tell she cannot hide when operating at full capacity.

Character Overview

Autafon "Amber" Deliamber learned early that being visibly decent does not make the world gentle. As a half-Chinese woman moving through the hard edges of respectability, bureaucracy, and ordinary prejudice, she grew into the habit of watchfulness long before she understood that some of what she noticed was not ordinary at all. People in pain drew her eye. Fear arrived in rooms before anyone spoke it aloud. Sickness, lies, spiritual wrongness, and the small disturbances that others ignored seemed to announce themselves to her whether she wanted them to or not.

The Lotus Bell did not make Amber into something she had never been. It gave discipline, language, and obligation to gifts that were already pushing at the edges of her life. Under their training she learned medicine, herbs, quiet spiritual practice, self-command, and the careful use of powers never meant to be displayed carelessly before the world. None of it came free. It tied her to a hidden order, made secrecy a permanent condition, and put enemies on her trail.

In the Dragon Breath build, the Lotus Bell training produced something with an edge that the other configurations express differently. She is a conscientious occult helper - healer, sensor, moral center - and she can also breathe fire at range fifty when the situation requires it. She dresses conservatively and keeps the shaded glasses on. The eyes are a tell she cannot fully manage.
