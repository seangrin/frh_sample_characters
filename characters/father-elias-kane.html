Father Elias Kane

Removed-but-not-defrocked urban priest-lawyer and exorcist who meets the dead as persons, not as threats - and will box before he shoots.

Points: 375 Age: 42 Public Read: A calm, methodical Catholic priest with a boxing background and the eyes of someone who has seen a confession go very wrong Core Role: Spiritual discerner, institutional investigator, exorcist, and pastoral presence at difficult deaths

Powers

  • True Faith [15] and Blessed 1 [10]: Elias carries genuine spiritual authority that goes beyond doctrine - a real metaphysical relationship that manifests most clearly when he confronts the unquiet dead or genuine evil.
  • Spiritual discernment: Empathy [15], Spirit Empathy [10], and Intuition [15] let him distinguish fraud, neurosis, malice, and the genuinely supernatural before anyone else has named what is wrong in a room.
  • Exorcism-17: the operational expression of his hidden-power lane. He does not treat the dead as mere threats. He relates to them as persons and burdens, which makes him both more dangerous and more careful than a blunt weapon against the supernatural.
  • Legal and institutional x-ray: Law-18, Administration-18, and an IQ 17 with multiple relevant skills make him one of the best institutional investigators in the FRH character set.

Required Secret Power

Blessed 1 [10] and True Faith [15]: genuine supernatural spiritual authority concealed within a priestly identity that appears credentialed but institutionally troubled.

Spirit Empathy [10] and Intuition [15]: perceptual gifts that function as occult discernment - he senses things he cannot always explain.

Exorcism-17 [4]: the practical operational skill that makes the hidden power dangerous in play.

Secret (Blessed / True Faith / occult discernment) [-20]: the real scope of his spiritual authority is hidden. If fully known, it would draw the wrong kind of institutional and supernatural attention simultaneously.

Required Secret Society

Patron (Doctrinist Correspondence Circle) (9 or less) [10].

The Doctrinist Correspondence Circle is Elias's non-FRH hidden-order lane. FRH does not count toward this requirement.

Secret (Doctrinist Correspondence Circle) [-5]. The Circle's existence and Elias's membership are both concealed.

Additional hidden layer: Secret (Removed from active ministry after failed exorcism) [-10] - this is not a society tie, but it is a compound institutional secret that makes his clerical position permanently unstable.

How Father Kane Uses His Powers

  • He reads rooms, confessions, hospitals, parishes, police desks, and boxing gyms for spiritual weight - the difference between ordinary human trouble and something that has left a mark on the dead or the place.
  • His Exorcism skill is pastoral first, aggressive only when necessary. He will try to understand what holds a spirit before trying to expel it. That patience is his greatest tactical advantage.
  • His institutional skills - Law, Administration, Research, Theology - make him the party's best navigator of Church archives, diocesan records, ecclesiastical politics, and official Catholic geography.
  • His failed exorcism backstory means he carries a known limit. He is not a guarantee. He is the most capable person available for this kind of work in 1923 America, and that is not the same thing as omnipotence.
  • Combat Reflexes and Boxing-15 mean he can function in a physical crisis, but will box before he shoots and will seek every other option first.

Why He Works

Father Kane proves that True Faith in a GURPS context reads best when it costs something - the failed exorcism, the removal from ministry, the hidden order, and the multiplying secrets make his spiritual authority genuinely dramatic rather than merely mechanical.

Character Overview

Father Elias Kane was already several men before the supernatural laid claim to him. He had studied law hard enough to understand how institutions hide, evade, and justify themselves. He had served in war long enough to know what fear does to prayer, to reason, and to a man's face when he thinks he is dying. He had boxed enough to learn discipline through pain. And somewhere under all of that he had still become a priest, not because the world was orderly, but because it plainly was not. By the time most people meet him he is removed from active ministry after a failed exorcism, but not defrocked, which leaves him in the uneasy position of being both inside the Church and not entirely trusted by it.

That unstable footing is part of what makes him useful. Elias moves comfortably through parish offices, church basements, police desks, hospital corridors, boxing gyms, and family kitchens where grief has begun to rot into fear. He understands records, doctrine, interrogation, confession, and the way desperate people lie when they want permission to keep lying. More importantly, he does not meet the dead merely as threats. He relates to them as persons and burdens. A haunting to him is not just evil to be struck down, but unfinished obligation, guilt, fear, and pressure lodged in a room or body until someone with enough courage and sacramental authority makes it answer properly.

His gifts are real, which is part of the trouble. Elias can discern too much, sense too much, and sometimes knows the difference between fraud, neurosis, malice, and the genuinely unquiet dead before anyone has spoken the thing aloud. That is why his secrets have multiplied around him: the failed exorcism, the hidden doctrinal circle, the spiritual authority he cannot publicly admit to possessing. He is still a priest, but he is no longer a simple public one. He is a priest-lawyer-investigator whose vocation now runs through difficult deaths, bad cases, compromised souls, and the sorts of rooms where doctrine alone is not enough unless the man carrying it can also take a punch.

For a new player, Elias should feel like a removed-but-not-defrocked urban priest who can pray, persuade, research, argue, and if forced, fight. He will box before he shoots. He will read the record before he trusts the rumor. And when the dead press too hard against the living, he is the kind of man who will answer them as a pastor first and an exorcist only when he has no kinder choice left.
