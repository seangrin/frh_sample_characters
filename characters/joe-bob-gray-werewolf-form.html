Alternate Form - Werewolf. This sheet documents Joe Bob Gray's werewolf state. Joe Bob's human form carries his secrets, his society, his identity, and whatever is left of his judgment. The GM runs this form. Joe Bob does not.

Joe Bob Gray

Werewolf Form

7'10", 350 lbs, IQ 7, Mute, Berserk, Bloodlust, and Amnesia (Doesn't Remember Being Human) - Regeneration 1 HT/turn except silver or magic weapons, and the ability to run at 34 mph in a straight line toward whatever the smell says is unfinished business.

Powers

  • Regeneration (1 HT/turn) [80], except Silver/Magic Weapons: the form heals a point of HT every turn under all conditions except those specific vulnerabilities. Conventional weapons, including all 1923-era firearms that are not silver-loaded, do not end this fight - they slow it. The only reliable way to put this form down is with silver ammunition or magically empowered attacks.
  • ST 28, Claws (Talons) [40] - imp 3d-1, cut 5d+1: the cutting output is catastrophic against any unarmored target. Most 1923-era civilians and many combatants have no meaningful protection against it.
  • Sharp Teeth [5] - cut 3d-1: a secondary bite attack that is itself heavier than most armed human close combat.
  • Super Running 1 [20] - 16.875 / 34 MPH: this form outruns automobiles at speed. Escape on foot is not an option. A party that needs to separate from this form needs transportation or terrain.
  • Immunity to Poison [15]: chemical deterrents, sedation attempts, and poison-based approaches all fail.
  • Enhanced senses: Acute Taste and Smell +1, Acute Vision +1, Acute Hearing +1, Alertness +3 - the form tracks by scent and sound with high reliability.

FRH Package

Note: The FRH package - Patron [60], Duty [-25], Enemy [-60] - is not present in the werewolf form's stat block. At IQ 7, Amnesia (Doesn't Remember Being Human), and Mute, the form cannot exercise or recognize institutional affiliations. The Secret Sons of the South connection and FRH obligations belong to the human Joe Bob and cannot be engaged from this state.

The Enemy (Unknown Enemies of FRH) disadvantage does not appear in this form's stats. Whether FRH's enemies recognize or target this form is a GM judgment - the form itself has no awareness of the relationship.

Required Secret Power

This sheet IS the required secret power.

The Alternate Form (Werewolf) [296] in Joe Bob's human form is the core mechanical secret. The human form carries Secret (Uncontrollable Werewolf) [-20] and Uncontrolled Change 4 [-50] as the cost of this state.

The [296] point Alternate Form advantage in the human sheet represents the full mechanical weight of this form. Discovery in 1923 America means imprisonment, mob violence, exile, or death. Joe Bob knows this. That is why the secrecy is not theatrical - it is existential.

Required Secret Society

Patron (Secret Sons of the South) (12 or less) [30] belongs to Joe Bob's human form and cannot be accessed in this state.

At IQ 7 and Amnesia (Doesn't Remember Being Human), the werewolf form has no awareness of the Secret Sons of the South, Joe Bob's human political commitments, or any of the social infrastructure of his human life. Those relationships exist in a body and mind this form cannot reach.

FRH does not satisfy the secret-society requirement.

How the Werewolf Form Operates

  • Amnesia (Doesn't Remember Being Human) means the form does not know who Joe Bob's allies are. Everyone in range is a potential target. The party has no exemption.
  • Regeneration 1 HT/turn means that wounding this form with conventional weapons only slows it while it heals. A party without silver or magical resources cannot put it down - they can only survive it or run.
  • Mute (Only Growls and Snarls) means no communication is possible. The form cannot be reasoned with, calmed with words, or redirected by appeal to Joe Bob's human relationships.
  • Berserk and Bloodlust mean that downed opponents will not be left alone. Separating the form from its targets requires physical removal or terrain, not persuasion.
  • IQ 7 means most human skills effectively disappear: Brawling-17 is the highest skill this form has, followed by Stealth-15 and Running-15. Tracking-10 takes 8 points to achieve at IQ 7 - the form is a poor tracker despite the sensory advantages, relying on smell more than learned skill.
  • The GM runs this form. Joe Bob is gone. Some part of his goals may remain in the form the way smell remains in a room after a fight - but it is not enough to steer what he becomes.

Advantages

Acute Hearing +1 [2]; Acute Taste and Smell +1 [2]; Acute Vision +1 [2]; Alertness +3 [15]; Claws (Talons) [40]; High Pain Threshold [10]; Immunity to Poison [15]; Regeneration (1 HT/turn) [80] (no healing from silver/magic weapons); Sharp Teeth [5]; Super Running 1 (16.875 / 34 MPH) [20].

Disadvantages

Amnesia (Doesn't Remember Being Human) [-25]; Berserk [-15]; Bloodlust [-10]; Monstrous [-25]; Mute (Only Growls and Snarls) [-25].

Note: Secret (Uncontrollable Werewolf), Uncontrolled Change 4, Code of Honor (Southern Gentlemen), Fanaticism, Secret (Is a White Supremacist / Confederacy Supporter), and FRH Duty/Enemy all belong to Joe Bob's human form. They do not appear in this form's stats.

Investigative Role

  • This form is not an investigative resource
  • Its activation is an investigative event - it generates consequences the human Joe Bob must manage afterward
  • Its existence proves the secrecy is not paranoia: in 1923, what this form is would end Joe Bob's life as a free person
  • The aftermath - physical evidence, witnesses, silver ammunition questions - is the case this form creates

Why It Works

The werewolf form earns its point cost not in combat utility but in dramatic weight. Joe Bob's real biography belongs to the human investigator who keeps trying to stay ahead of the monster. The form's Amnesia and Mute confirm that the curse is not a second polished identity - it is the catastrophic expression of the same life. Making the curse this honest is what gives the human side room to be a believable PI instead of a delivery system for lycanthropy.

Skills

Brawling-17 [4]; Climbing-14 [1]; Jumping-15 [1]; Running (Move: 16.875)-15 [4]; Stealth-15 [2]; Tracking-10 [8].

All skills are at IQ 7 base. Tracking-10 requiring 8 points illustrates how completely the form's animalistic drive outpaces learned skill. The enhanced senses do the tracking work that the skill cannot.

Character Overview

The werewolf form is not Joe Bob Gray. It is what happens when Joe Bob Gray stops existing for a while. From an early age he learned that anger, fear, or the wrong sort of pressure could strip him out of his human mind and throw him into an animal state that remembers very little except hunger, violence, and the crooked instinct to keep moving toward unfinished business. He did not grow into the curse gracefully. He grew around it the way men grow around a shotgun scar.

The form itself is not evil in any organized sense. It is a predator at IQ 7 with no memory of what it was and no language for what it wants. It tracks by smell, attacks what moves toward it and what moves away, and heals at a rate that makes conventional intervention useless. The Bloodlust means it does not leave things half-finished. That is the danger in a dense environment - not that it is strategic, but that it is thorough.

For a GM, this form should be treated as weather: it arrives, it does what weather does, and then it leaves, and everyone who survived is left deciding what to do about what it touched. Joe Bob's investigative value is entirely in his human form. The werewolf is the price of that value.
