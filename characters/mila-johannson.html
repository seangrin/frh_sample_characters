Mila Johannson

Unaging Amazon monster hunter - legendary in body, practical in mind, and too old for her face by about a century of pursuit, sacred equipment, and concealed vocation.

Powers

  • Unaging [15] with true age 128: Mila has not aged normally. Whatever the Amazon tradition produced in her, it suspended the ordinary rules of time in a body that has had over a century to accumulate hunting experience, sacred equipment, and operational damage.
  • Regeneration (1 HT/min) [35]: she heals at a rate that makes ordinary injury a temporary setback rather than a crisis - with the important limitation that regeneration does not apply to wounds from magic or certain monsters.
  • ST 22/13, HT 13/22, DR 10, Gigantism: she is physically far beyond any normal human. At 7'11" and 380 lbs with superhuman strength and toughness, she reads as impossible even before the equipment enters the picture.
  • Magic equipment suite: Magic Broadsword, Magic Spear, Magic Shield, Magic Breastplate, Magic Greathelm, Magic Gauntlets, Magic Boots, Set of Common Rune Stones. Her armament is as unusual as her body.

Required Secret Power

Unaging Amazon hunter physique (grouped):

  • Unaging [15] - the anchor of the hidden lane; 128 years old with no natural aging
  • Regeneration (1 HT/min) [35] - recovery rate no normal human body can match (caveat: does not apply to magic wounds or certain monsters)
  • ST 22/13, HT 13/22 - superhuman physical stats
  • DR 10 (Everything) [30] - exceptional damage resistance
  • Unusual Background (Unaging Monster Hunter) [20]

Secret Identity (Unaging Monster Hunter) [-20]: her true nature - both the impossible age and the full scale of her vocation - must be concealed. Discovery would draw scientific exploitation, institutional classification, and supernatural targeting.

Required Secret Society

Amazon Monster Hunters. The hidden tradition that produced Mila - an order of women hunters carrying an older vocation against monsters the modern world prefers not to acknowledge.

Secret (Member of Amazon Monster Hunters) [-20].

Note: The source sheet does not list a formal Patron advantage for this organization. The society tie is expressed entirely through the Secret disadvantage rather than a named Patron. This appears consistent with her design - the tradition she belongs to may not organize around formal institutional patronage in the same way other hidden orders do. If a formal Patron entry should be added, this would need human review.

FRH does not satisfy the secret-society requirement.

How Mila Uses Her Powers

  • She is the party's monster specialist - the person whose vocation is literally the elimination of things that should not be allowed to continue existing. Her century of experience means she has seen most of the things the party will encounter before.
  • Her Regeneration means she can absorb damage and return to action faster than any other party member - as long as the damage is not from magic or specific monster capabilities. The caveat matters and creates genuine tactical risk.
  • Her magic equipment suite (spear, shield, sword, full armor) is operationally superior to anything commercially available in 1923. Each piece implies a history and a custodial relationship with her tradition.
  • Her Hidden Lore (Monsters)-12 means she is not just physically capable against monsters - she has actual knowledge about their nature, weaknesses, and behavior patterns.
  • Her size, beauty, and unusual presence mean she cannot move incognito in most contexts. She is always the most visible person in the room, which creates constraints that require the party to plan around her rather than treating her as a flexible operative.

Why She Works

Mila proves that legendary physical capability reads best when it comes with a century of secrecy and operational discipline attached. She is not there to be celebrated. She is there to track, endure, survive, and kill the thing that should not have been allowed to keep living.

Character Overview

Mila Johannson walks through the modern world as if history had forgotten to finish with her. She is too old for her face, too large for ordinary comfort, too durable for common explanation, and too practiced in violence against monsters to mistake any of that for a blessing. Whatever hidden Amazon tradition produced her, it produced a huntress rather than a scholar: a woman shaped by long pursuit, sacred equipment, bodily discipline, and the hard knowledge that some things in the world do not deserve diplomacy before they deserve a spear. Her gear, scars, and instincts all imply a life spent treating monstrosity as work.

What makes Mila distinctive is that she is not a myth in a vacuum. She must still move through cities, trains, hotels, alleys, and committee-made modernity while concealing both her impossible age and the true scale of her vocation. The secrecy around her is double. She carries the hidden identity of an unaging monster hunter, and she belongs to an older covert hunting tradition that the ordinary world would regard as absurd until it saw what her weapons are meant to stop. Beauty, size, and social difficulty complicate every room she enters. People stare, speculate, submit, or recoil long before they understand why.

For a new player, Mila should feel legendary in body but practical in mind. She is not there to solve a salon conversation or parse a dusty archive first. She is there to track, endure, survive, and kill the thing that should not have been allowed to keep living. The gift of the character is that all of that ferocity sits inside a woman who has had to stay hidden for more than a century and therefore knows exactly how much patience, restraint, and secrecy are required to keep a life going after the world has already become unnatural around you.
